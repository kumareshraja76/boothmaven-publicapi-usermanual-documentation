## Chapter 7: Stories

Stories are short-form, social-style content pieces — similar to Instagram Stories — that staff can share with visitors on the show floor. Great for product demos, behind-the-scenes clips, and event highlights. Managed under **My Pages → Stories**.

### 7.1 The Stories Library

Displays all created stories with name, status, and creation date. Toggle published status, create new stories, or edit existing ones. Staff can also view and share stories from the Mobile App.

![Stories library — all stories with published status](/manual/manager/images/img_19.png)

*▲ Stories library — all stories with published status*

### 7.2 Creating a Story

Click **Add New Story**. Give it a title and add slides — each slide can contain an image, short video, or text overlay. Reorder slides by dragging. Set to **Published** so staff can share it from the mobile app.

![Create Story — add slides with images, video, or text overlays](/manual/manager/images/img_20.png)
