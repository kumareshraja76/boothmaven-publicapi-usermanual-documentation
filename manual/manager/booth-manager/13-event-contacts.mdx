## Chapter 13: Reviewing Event Contacts

Navigate to **Contacts** to see all leads from your assigned events. Use the Hot / Warm / Cool filter tabs to focus on the highest-priority leads first. Click **View** on any row to see their full profile, qualification answers, notes, and meeting history.

Create contacts manually via **Create Contact** — useful when you collect a business card and want to log it directly in the web app.

<Tip>
  After the show, review Cool leads too. Some may be better qualified than their
  score suggests if staff ran out of time to complete the qualification
  questions.
</Tip>
