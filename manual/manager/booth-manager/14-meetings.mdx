## Chapter 14: Working with Meetings

Your Meetings view shows all meetings across your assigned events. Filter by status to see what needs attention. Book new meetings using **+ Book Meeting** (see Chapter 8 for steps), or update an existing meeting's status after it occurs.

The **RSVP tab** in Meetings shows leads who have responded to meeting invitations — useful when you have a pre-event scheduling flow active before the show.
