## Chapter 15: Sharing Digital Assets (Web)

Share digital cards, content, and microsites with contacts directly from the web:

1. Go to **Contacts** and select one or more contacts.
2. Click **Bulk Actions** and choose _Share Digital Card_ or _Share Content_.
3. Select the card or asset to share.
4. Selected contacts receive an email with a link to the shared asset.

<Tip>
  Share a product-specific digital card with leads who expressed interest in
  that product, rather than your general business card. It creates a more
  relevant, personalised experience.
</Tip>
