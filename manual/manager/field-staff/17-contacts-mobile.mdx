## Chapter 17: Managing Contacts on Mobile

The Contacts tab shows all leads captured at the current event. Search, filter, view profiles, add notes, answer qualification questions, and perform bulk actions.

### 17.1 The Contacts List

Tap the **Contacts** tab. Each card shows name, job title, event tag, lead score badge, and a visual score bar. Toggle between *Contacts* and *Lists* at the top. Use the search bar or **Filters** to narrow results.

![Contacts list — all leads with scores, event tags, and quick-action menus](/manual/manager/images/img_31.jpg)

*▲ Contacts list — all leads with scores, event tags, and quick-action menus*

### 17.2 Viewing a Contact Profile

Tap any contact card to open their full profile, organised into five tabs:

* **Profile** — Contact information, upcoming meeting status, and quick actions: Enrich, Book Meeting, More.
* **Qualification** — Answers to your booth questions, quality rating, and total score.
* **Notes** — Free-text and voice notes.
* **Meetings** — All meetings linked to this contact.
* **More** — Additional actions and contact history.

![Contact Profile — tabs for Profile, Qualification, Notes, Meetings, and quick-action buttons](/manual/manager/images/img_32.jpg)

*▲ Contact Profile — tabs for Profile, Qualification, Notes, Meetings, and quick-action buttons*

### 17.3 Answering Qualification Questions

Tap the **Qualification** tab on a contact profile to see your booth questions and answer them. The lead quality status (Hot, Warm, or Cool) and total score update automatically as you answer. A progress bar shows how many questions have been answered.

![Qualification tab — answer booth questions to update lead score automatically](/manual/manager/images/img_33.jpg)

*▲ Qualification tab — answer booth questions to update lead score automatically*

<Tip>
Always come back to fill in qualification questions before the end of the day. Qualified leads are significantly more useful to your sales team during follow-up.
</Tip>

### 17.4 Adding Notes

Tap the **Notes** tab. Existing notes appear as cards with timestamps. Tap **+ Add Text Note** to type an observation, or tap **Record Voice** to leave a spoken note. Notes are visible to all team members with access to that event.

![Notes tab — text notes with timestamps; add text or voice notes](/manual/manager/images/img_34.jpg)

*▲ Notes tab — text notes with timestamps; add text or voice notes*

### 17.5 Bulk Actions in Contacts

Long-press any contact card to enter selection mode (checkboxes appear). Select contacts, then tap the bulk action menu. Available actions:

* **Add to List** — Group contacts into an existing or new list.
* **Delete** — Remove selected contacts (cannot be undone).
* **Share Digital Card** — Send your card to all selected contacts.
* **Share Content** — Send a digital asset to all selected contacts.
* **Export** — Export selected contacts as a file.
* **Add Tag** — Apply a shared label to multiple contacts.
