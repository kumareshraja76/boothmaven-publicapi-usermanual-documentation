## Chapter 4: Engagement Analytics & Intelligence

Capturing a lead is only the first step; understanding what products, documents, and videos that lead engaged with after leaving your booth is what closes deals. BoothMaven's **Analytics & Insights** module (/tracking/analytics-and-insights) provides deep, real-time engagement intelligence broken down across two vital dimensions: **Engagement by Contact** and **Engagement by Content**.

### 4.1 High-Level Performance Metrics

The analytics overview banner displays core performance indicators across your published digital assets:

- **Total Views** — Overall aggregate views across all digital business cards, product pages, and microsites.
- **Engagement Rate (%)** — Percentage of visitors who took active follow-up actions (downloaded PDFs, clicked outbound links, watched videos, booked meetings).
- **Average Time Spent** — Mean session duration attendees spent exploring your digital materials.
- **Shares & Downloads** — Total PDF downloads and peer-to-peer card shares executed by visitors.
- **Known Contacts vs Anonymous Views** — Distinguishes between identified leads with contact profiles and anonymous traffic.

### 4.2 Engagement Analytics by Contact

Under the **Overview / Contact Engagement** tab, BoothMaven tracks how each individual prospect interacts with your materials over time. This transforms passive contact records into prioritized, intent-driven sales opportunities.

#### Contact Engagement Metrics & Tiers

- **Engagement Score (0–100)** — A dynamic score calculated based on the frequency, recency, and depth of content interactions.
- **Engagement Tier Badges**:
      🔥 **Hot** — Highly engaged prospects with frequent return visits, multiple document downloads, or meeting requests. Prioritize for immediate sales calls.
- ✏️ **Warm** — Moderate interest; prospect viewed collateral once or twice and explored product links.
- ⚙️ **Cool** — Low engagement; minimal interaction following the initial booth scan. Ideal for nurture sequences.

  **Views & Actions Count** — Exact count of page opens and high-value actions (brochure downloads, link clicks) triggered by that specific contact.
  **Event Tag & Last Active** — Associated trade show and relative timestamp of their most recent interaction (e.g. _"2 hours ago"_).
  **Direct Quick Actions** — Click **View** to open their complete profile or click **Reach Out** to initiate a direct follow-up email or call while their interest is fresh.
  **Filter & Sort** — Filter contacts by _All, Hot, Warm_, or _Cool_; sort by _Most Engaged, Most Recent_, or _Most Views_.

### 4.3 Engagement Analytics by Content

The **Content Activity** tab flips the lens from _who_ is looking to _what_ is being looked at. It provides a granular, visit-by-visit audit of how attendees consume your digital cards, brochures, and microsites.

#### Granular Visit Session Stream

Content activity is grouped by distinct visitor sessions:

- **Visitor Identification** — Displays the visitor's name, company, and avatar for _Known Contacts_, or an _Anonymous Visitor_ indicator when an unregistered user explores a public QR code.
- **Expandable Activity Breakdown** — Click any session card to expand the full chronological log of micro-actions within that visit:
      *"Viewed Business Card"* (with eye icon and timestamp)
- _"Downloaded Product Brochure PDF"_ (with download icon and timestamp)
- _"Clicked Company Website URL"_ (with external link icon and timestamp)
- _"Played Product Demo Video"_ (with video icon and duration)

  **Anonymous Visitor Intelligence** — Anonymous scans still provide valuable insights on booth footfall and asset popularity, showing which physical flyers or booth displays generated the highest traffic volume.

Review Content Analytics post-show to see which brochures and video demos drew the highest dwell time, helping marketing refine collateral for future expos.
