## Chapter 15: Getting Started on the Mobile App

### 15.1 Downloading the BoothMaven App

Install the BoothMaven app on your smartphone before the event. The app is available for both iOS and Android:

* 🍎 [Download on the App Store (iOS)](https://apps.apple.com/app/boothmaven) https://apps.apple.com/app/boothmaven
* 🤖 [Get it on Google Play (Android)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/boothmaven) https://play.google.com/store/apps/boothmaven

<Note>
You will need your login credentials from your Account Admin before you can sign in. Confirm your email and password are set up before the event.
</Note>

### 15.2 Logging In

Open the app. Enter the email address and password provided by your Account Admin, then tap the green **LOGIN** button.

If your admin has set you up as a sub-user (a shared booth account), tap **Login as subuser** below the main button and enter the sub-user credentials instead.

![Login screen — enter your credentials or use sub-user login](/manual/staff/images/img_25.jpg)

*▲ Login screen — enter your credentials or use sub-user login*

<Tip>
If you forget your password, tap **Forgot Password** on the login screen to receive a reset link by email.
</Tip>

<Note>
The app language can be changed from the selector at the bottom of the login screen before you log in.
</Note>

### 15.3 Navigating the App

Once logged in, the main navigation bar at the bottom has five tabs:

* **Capture** — Scan or manually enter leads.
* **Contacts** — View and manage all captured contacts.
* **Meetings** — Book and track all meetings.
* **Share** — Share your digital card, content, team cards, and microsites.
* **Stories** — Browse and share your company's stories.

Tap the menu icon (≡) at the top left for the side drawer: quick access to Contacts, Share, Stories, QR, Card Scanner, Settings, and Logout, plus summary tiles for Lists and Stories counts.

![Side drawer — navigation, summary stats, and account settings](/manual/staff/images/img_26.jpg)

*▲ Side drawer — navigation, summary stats, and account settings*

<Note>
The app works offline for lead capture and contact viewing. Changes made offline sync automatically once your phone reconnects to the internet.
</Note>
