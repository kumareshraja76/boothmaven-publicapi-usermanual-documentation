## Chapter 18: Booking & Managing Meetings on Mobile

The Meetings tab gives a real-time view of all meetings scheduled for your current event. Book new meetings, view details, reschedule, cancel, and record outcomes.

### 18.1 The Meetings Screen

Tap the **Meetings** tab. Each meeting card shows contact name, job title and company, date/time, format, and current status. Filter tabs at the top: **All, Scheduled, Rescheduled, Done**, and **Cancelled**.

![Meetings screen — all meetings with status, date, time, type, and location](/manual/staff/images/img_35.jpg)

*▲ Meetings screen — all meetings with status, date, time, type, and location*

### 18.2 Booking a Meeting from Mobile

Tap the **+** button in the top-right corner. Fill in the meeting title, contact, date/time, duration, type (In-Person or Virtual), and location. Tap **Save**.

A confirmation screen appears. From here you can share meeting details with others via email, WhatsApp, your device's email app, or a general share sheet.

![Meeting Booked confirmation — share details via email, WhatsApp, or device sharing](/manual/staff/images/img_36.jpg)

*▲ Meeting Booked confirmation — share details via email, WhatsApp, or device sharing*

### 18.3 Viewing Meeting Details

Tap any meeting card to open the full Meeting Detail view: current status badge, title, event and booth, contact name and company, and scheduling details. Three action buttons let you **Update Status**, **Reschedule**, or **Cancel** the meeting.

![Meeting Detail — full information with Update Status, Reschedule, and Cancel actions](/manual/staff/images/img_37.jpg)

*▲ Meeting Detail — full information with Update Status, Reschedule, and Cancel actions*

### 18.4 Updating a Meeting Status

Tap **Update Status** on the meeting detail screen. Choose what happened:

* **Done** — The meeting took place as planned.
* **Rescheduled** — The meeting was moved (you will be prompted to set the new time).
* **Cancelled** — The meeting was cancelled.
* **No Show** — The contact did not attend.

Select an **Outcome** from the dropdown and add key discussion points in the Notes field. Tap **Save Status Update**.

![Update Status — record meeting outcome with notes](/manual/staff/images/img_38.jpg)

*▲ Update Status — record meeting outcome with notes*

<Tip>
Always update meeting statuses on the same day. This data feeds directly into your meeting reports and helps your team prioritise follow-up after the show.
</Tip>
