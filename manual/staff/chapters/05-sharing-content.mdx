## Chapter 19: Sharing Digital Content on Mobile

The Share tab lets you distribute your digital presence to leads on the show floor — no paper required.

### 19.1 Share Screen Overview

Tap the **Share** tab. Your name and role appear at the top with a _"Works offline"_ badge — you can share even without an internet connection. Four sharing options are available:

![Share screen — four sharing options: My Card, Content, Team Cards, and Microsites](/manual/staff/images/img_39.jpg)

_▲ Share screen — four sharing options: My Card, Content, Team Cards, and Microsites_

### 19.2 Sharing Your Digital Card

Tap **Share My Card** to display your personal digital card's QR code. The lead scans the QR code with their phone camera to open your card in their browser. Tap **Share Link** to send the card URL via any messaging app or email.

<Tip>
  Ask leads to scan your QR code while you are still talking with them at the
  booth. This is far more reliable than asking them to look you up later.
</Tip>

### 19.3 Sharing Content

Tap **Share Content** to browse your digital card library. Content is organised by type tabs: Professional, Business, Audio, Video, and more. Tap any item to share it via QR code or direct link.

![Share Content — browse all published cards and assets by type](/manual/staff/images/img_40.jpg)

_▲ Share Content — browse all published cards and assets by type_

### 19.4 Sharing Team Cards

Tap **Share Team Cards** to access your colleagues' digital cards — useful when a visitor asks about a team member not present at the booth.

### 19.5 Sharing Microsites

Tap **Microsites** to access all published interactive landing pages. Select a microsite and present its QR code to the visitor. Ideal for sharing event-specific product pages or detailed company information.
